The [MIT licence](LICENSE) covers the material this project actually authored:
the lecture notes themselves — prose, mathematical exposition, structure — along
with the build configuration, themes, scripts, and the diagrams drawn for this
project.

It does **not** cover third-party material reproduced inside those notes.

## Figures

Diagrams in these notes are drawn by scripts committed beside them
(`figures/<lecture>/make_diagrams.py`) and are original work covered by the MIT
licence. Where a figure is reproduced from a paper or another publication, its
copyright remains with the original authors and publishers; it is included
under a good-faith understanding of fair use for non-commercial educational
purposes, credited in its caption, and no licence to it is granted by this
project.

Every figure is listed in [`figures/SOURCES.md`](figures/SOURCES.md), which
records where it came from and how that was established.

## Lecture recordings and class notes

The notes are written from Stanford's publicly available CS229 lecture
recordings and refer readers to the official class notes. Neither the
recordings, their transcripts, nor the official notes are redistributed here;
the chapters link to them instead.
